# Review Management

The review module helps sellers manage customer reviews and feedback. It tracks orders, review requests, and customer sentiment over time.

---

## What You Will Learn

- How the review dashboard shows order and review statistics
- How review charts compare different time periods
- How review request tracking works
- How the review compare chart helps with analysis

---

## Review Dashboard

The review dashboard shows key metrics:
- **Total Orders** — Number of orders in the selected period
- **Reviews Requested** — Total review requests sent
- **Review Response Rate** — Percentage of orders that resulted in reviews
- **Average Rating** — Average star rating across all reviews
- **Review Distribution** — Breakdown by star rating (1-5 stars)

---

## Review Charts

### Review Statistic Chart

Shows review trends over time:
- Reviews received per day/week/month
- Average rating trend
- Review volume compared to previous period

### Review Compare Chart

Compares review metrics between two periods:
- Current period vs. previous period
- Current period vs. same period last year
- Side-by-side comparison of ratings distribution

---

## Review Request Tracking

The system tracks when review requests were sent and outcomes:
- Orders that received review requests
- Orders that resulted in reviews
- Response time (how long after request did customer leave a review)
- Review content (positive, neutral, negative)

---

## Interview Talking Points

**On reviews as a business driver:** "Reviews directly impact sales. A product dropping from 4.5 to 3.5 stars can lose sales overnight. The compare chart makes two data calls (current period and comparison period) and renders them side by side with matched date alignments. This lets sellers see if operational changes are actually improving review scores over time."
