# Tips System

The tips system delivers business optimization suggestions to sellers. Tips are generated based on data analysis and delivered through the notification system alongside alerts.

---

## What You Will Learn

- How tips are generated
- How tips are different from alerts
- How tips are displayed in the UI
- How tips are managed (view, dismiss, apply)

---

## Tips vs Alerts

| Aspect    | Alerts                          | Tips                        |
| --------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Purpose   | Notify about problems            | Suggest improvements        |
| Urgency   | Time-sensitive                   | Informational               |
| Source    | System monitoring                | Data analysis               |
| Action    | Often requires immediate action  | Optional recommendation     |

---

## Tip Types

Tips cover various optimization areas:

- **Product pricing** - Suggestions for price adjustments based on market conditions
- **Inventory management** - Recommendations for stock replenishment timing
- **Advertising** - Suggestions for PPC campaign improvements
- **Cost optimization** - Recommendations for reducing fees or costs
- **Seasonal planning** - Suggestions based on upcoming seasonal trends

---

## Tip Display

Tips are displayed alongside alerts in the notification system. They appear in:

- The notification panel with a tip icon (lightbulb)
- The tips list page for browsing all tips
- The dashboard tips counter widget

Each tip shows:
- Title
- Description with actionable advice
- Category tag
- Timestamp
- Action button (where applicable)

---

## Interview Talking Points

**On the tips data analysis:** "Tips are generated by looking at seller data patterns. For example, the system identifies products with high sales velocity and low ad spend to suggest increasing PPC investment. Or it spots seasonal trends from last year and suggests preparing inventory ahead of time. The logic is rule-based and runs as a background job, same as alert monitoring."
